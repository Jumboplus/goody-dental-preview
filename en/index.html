How Social Security pays

Can I use Thai Social Security here?

If you are insured under Thai Social Security (Section 33 or 39), the dental cover is 900 baht a year for scaling, fillings and extractions. Since 1 May 2026 there is also a separate limit for wisdom tooth surgery: up to 1,500 baht per tooth for simple cases and up to 2,500 baht per tooth when bone has to be removed. If the clinic is a Social Security contract provider, it bills Social Security directly and you pay nothing up front within the limit; if not, you pay first and claim the money back through SSO e-Self Service or an SSO office. Ask us on LINE what to bring.

Does scaling hurt?

Most people feel vibration or sensitivity rather than pain. If there is a lot of tartar or the gums are inflamed you may feel more. Tell the dentist during the cleaning and they can pause.
